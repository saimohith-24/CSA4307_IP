# FoneBill - Monthly Postpaid Phone Bill & Auto-Pay Portal

## Aim
To develop a telecom postpaid billing portal ('FoneBill') displaying monthly billing cycles, overdue fine calculation (₹10/day), full or custom partial payment options, and automated payment reminders.

## Procedure
1. Display customer postpaid account summary with current due amount, billing period, and overdue penalty calculation (₹10/day).
2. Allow full or custom partial payments via UPI/Card/Wallet with instant receipt and billing history updates.
3. Provide an Admin Monthly Billing Cycle & Due Date controller.

## How to Run
Open `index.html` directly in any web browser.
